04 /Find matching flightsAudio fingerprints only

Find a content match

Compare distinctive audio landmarks and repeated 3 second audio sections. Camera dates can differ; they do not need to be correct.

A seed percentage samples one feed evenly and searches all candidates on the other. Increase the audio range if the useful sound falls outside the first and last 30 seconds. Saved fingerprints are reused.

Suggest the rest after confirmation

Every confirmed pair teaches the difference between camera clocks, whether found by audio, dates or manual alignment. The app uses modified dates, recording durations and the confirmed sync offset to suggest related files and mark date agreement on audio candidates.

Copied or edited file dates may be unreliable. Filename dates are assumed to represent recording starts. Timestamp suggestions require review and never count as an audio match.
